That’s usually enough to reproduce a problem on the first try. Messages go straight to the person who wrote the code, so there’s no ticket queue between you and a fix.

Common questions

Open the App Store, tap your account icon in the top corner, then tap Purchased. PaverLab is in that list; tap the download icon next to it. There’s no charge, because the purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to the device.

Apple handles refunds for every app sold through the App Store, and Westmar can’t issue one directly. Request it at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Each app is covered on its own in the Privacy Policy, including what stays on your device and what, if anything, reaches Westmar.
