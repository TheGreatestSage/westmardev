Support

Every Westmar app is supported by the people who wrote it. There is one channel — email — and a person reads it.

Contact

Email hello@westmardev.com. Put the app name in the subject line, and include three things in the message:

  • The app — which Westmar app you are writing about.
  • Your iOS version — found under Settings → General → About.
  • Your device — the iPhone or iPad model you are using.

That is usually enough to reproduce a problem on the first try. Messages go directly to the person who wrote the code, so there is no ticket queue between you and a fix.

Common questions

How do I reinstall PaverLab on a new device?

Open the App Store, tap your account icon in the top corner, then tap Purchased. PaverLab is in that list — tap the download icon next to it. There is no charge; the purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to the device.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for every app sold through the App Store, and Westmar cannot issue one directly. Request it at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What does an app collect about me?

Each app is covered on its own in the Privacy Policy, app by app, including what stays on your device and what — if anything — reaches Westmar.
