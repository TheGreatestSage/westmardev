Privacy Policy

This policy covers every app published by Westmar LLC. The short version: each app collects only what it needs to work, keeps that data to itself, and none of it is ever sold or handed to advertisers.

Who we are

Westmar LLC is a Florida limited liability company operated by Nicholas Mello, its sole owner and developer. Westmar builds and publishes native iOS applications, including SteerJobs, YourBPM, Voyager: Cosmic Odometer, and PaverLab.

For anything in this policy — questions, requests, or complaints — email nmello427@gmail.com. Messages go directly to the person who wrote the code.

What each app collects

Westmar apps do not share a common account system. Each app collects its own data for its own purpose, and that list is specific to the app you are using.

SteerJobs

  • Account information — the email address used to sign in.
  • Job site photos — images you capture to document and approve work phases.
  • Location — GPS coordinates recorded at check-in, used to confirm presence on a job site. Location is captured only at the moment you check in, not continuously in the background.
  • Work records — hours, phase status, and job progress you enter.

YourBPM

  • Heart-rate data — read from Apple Health with your permission, to match playlist tempo to your current zone.
  • Music service connection — the authorization needed to read your library and control playback.

Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties — a restriction Apple imposes on HealthKit apps and one Westmar applies as policy regardless.

Voyager: Cosmic Odometer

  • Location and motion — used on-device to calculate your speed and distance travelled through space.

PaverLab

PaverLab has not yet shipped. Its data practices will be documented here before release.

Each app's data stays isolated

Westmar apps do not talk to each other. There is no shared login, no shared profile, and no combined dataset across apps. Data you enter into SteerJobs is not visible to YourBPM, and nothing collected in one app is used to build a picture of you across the others.

Deleting an app removes its on-device data. Data held on a server, where an app uses one, is removed on request as described below.

What is never done with your data

  • Your data is never sold, to anyone, for any price.
  • Your data is never shared with advertisers, ad networks, or data brokers.
  • Westmar apps contain no third-party advertising SDKs and do not run behavioural ad tracking.
  • Your data is not used to train machine learning models.

These are not aspirations subject to a future policy update — they are the operating rules across every Westmar app.

Third-party services

A small number of outside services are involved in distributing and running the apps. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job.

  • Apple — distributes the apps through the App Store and TestFlight and handles any purchases. Apple provides aggregate, anonymised download and crash statistics; it does not give Westmar the identity of individual users. Apple's own privacy policy governs that data.
  • TestFlight — used for beta distribution, currently for SteerJobs. If you join a beta, Apple shares your email address with Westmar so the build can be delivered to you, along with crash logs and any feedback you choose to submit.

Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can ask Westmar to:

  • Delete your data — have the data associated with your use of an app erased.
  • Get a copy of your data — receive an export of what an app holds about you, in a readable format.
  • Correct your data — fix anything inaccurate.

Email nmello427@gmail.com with the app name in the subject line. Requests are answered within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

Children's privacy

Westmar apps are not directed at children under 13, and Westmar does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Westmar app, email nmello427@gmail.com and it will be deleted promptly.

Security

Data is protected with industry-standard measures appropriate to its sensitivity, including encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, and no absolute guarantee is offered — but the amount of data collected is deliberately kept small, which is the most effective protection available.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes — anything that meaningfully affects what is collected or how it is used — will additionally be announced through an in-app notice or an email to affected users before the change takes effect.

Previous versions are available on request. Continuing to use a Westmar app after a change takes effect means the revised policy applies to you.
